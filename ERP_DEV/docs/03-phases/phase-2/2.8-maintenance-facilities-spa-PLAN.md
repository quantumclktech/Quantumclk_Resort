## 2. Service desk (maintenance + guest requests)

### 2.1 Ticket model
| Field | Values / rule |
|---|---|
| number | `SR-<yyyy>-000123` (readable; gaps allowed, not a money series) |
| type | MAINTENANCE · GUEST_REQUEST · PREVENTIVE |
| category → department | ELECTRICAL, PLUMBING, AC_HVAC, CARPENTRY_FABRICATION, WELDING, CIVIL, POOL, IT_AV, HOUSEKEEPING_ITEM (towels, amenities), LAUNDRY, WAKE_UP_CALL, F&B_REQUEST, OTHER. The mapping is a setting (Q-107/Q-111) |
| location | a room (from 2.3) **or** an area from a location list (lobby, kitchen, pool deck, Vedika Hall, …) + an optional asset text (e.g. "AC unit 2") |
| links | stay_ref (guest requests), facility (2.8 §3), parent PM plan |
| reported by | staff member (+ "on behalf of guest" with the stay) |
| priority | **P1 Urgent** (safety, or no power/water in an occupied room), **P2 High**, **P3 Normal**, **P4 Low**. Proposed from the category and occupancy; staff can change it with a reason |
| SLA | response and resolution targets per type × priority (placeholders P1 15 min / 2 h, P2 30 min / 4 h, P3 4 h / 24 h, P4 24 h / 72 h; Q-108). The clock **pauses in ON_HOLD** |
| status | NEW → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → ON_HOLD (parts / vendor / guest) → RESOLVED → CLOSED (automatically after N hours unless reopened, or confirmed by the reporter). **REOPENED** from RESOLVED/CLOSED within N days. CANCELLED with a reason |
| work | assignee (staff) **or** vendor visit, resolution notes, before/after photos, time spent, cost (paise), checklist (PM) |
| history | append-only status and comment log; audit for priority/SLA changes and cancellations |

### 2.2 Flows
- **Create:** any staff member, from the ERP web or phone (and from the 2.4 housekeeper "Report issue" button). The department queue gets it, with a notification for P1/P2.
- **Assign:** a department supervisor assigns a technician or a vendor. Technicians may pick from their department queue (a setting). Technician phone view: **My tickets** → Start / Hold (reason) / Resolve (notes + photo).
- **Room impact (2.3):** "Room can't be sold" on a ticket → a **block request** (OOS straight away; OOO through the 2.3 stock saga with manager approval). On resolve → the system asks to release the block, and the room returns to DIRTY for cleaning.
- **Facility impact:** a ticket can put a facility into MAINTENANCE status (§3), shown on the facility board and to reception.
- **Damage charge to a guest** (optional, Q-116): manager approval → a 2.5 charge on the stay's folio with the ticket as sourceRef.
- **Escalation job** (ShedLock, every minute):
  - response or resolution target missed → level 1, the department supervisor;
  - still breached after another target period → level 2, the **GM**.
  - Notifications are in-app + email. Each escalation is logged on the ticket.
- **Guest requests (D-179):** reception logs them against a stay (towels, pillows, laundry pickup, wake-up call, room issue). Routing:
  - towels, amenities and laundry → the housekeeping queue; they appear in the housekeeper's phone view next to their 2.4 tasks;
  - wake-up call → a due-time ticket for front office with an alert;
  - room issue → maintenance.
  - Closing it adds an entry to the guest's CRM timeline (2.6).

### 2.3 Vendors (D-178)
- `vendor`: name, trade(s), contact, GSTIN, notes, active.
- `vendor_visit` on a ticket: scheduled at, arrived, done, quoted cost → **manager approval above a limit** (Q-109) → actual cost, vendor invoice ref.
- No payments here (accounting is export only). Costs go into the maintenance reports and exports.

### 2.4 Preventive maintenance (D-178)
- `pm_plan`: name, department, location/asset text, **checklist items**, frequency (daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly / every N days), due time, priority, active.
- A job creates **PREVENTIVE** tickets when they fall due, with the checklist. Each item is ticked or failed, and a failed item can raise a follow-up ticket.
- **Missed-PM report.** The plan list (e.g. daily pool water test, AC servicing, generator check, fire safety) comes from the client (Q-110). Nothing is invented.

## 3. Facilities (pool, kids pool, gym, activities, games, venues)

### 3.1 Model
| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| `facility` | code, name, type (POOL, KIDS_POOL, GYM, SPA, ACTIVITY, GAMES, LIBRARY, KIDS_AREA, VENUE), location, status OPEN / CLOSED / MAINTENANCE (+ ticket link, reason), capacity, safety notes + "acknowledgement needed" flag (e.g. zip line), `needs_session` |
| `facility_hours` | per weekday, with exceptions (holidays) |
| `facility_access_rule` | user type (IN_HOUSE_GUEST, MEMBER + tier, DAY_VISITOR) → allowed?, price_paise or INCLUDED, max pax, age rule text |
| `facility_session` + `session_booking` | for slot facilities: date, start, end, capacity, booked. Booking for a stay/member/visitor with pax. **Capacity checked atomically** (`booked + pax ≤ capacity`). BOOKED → CHECKED_IN / NO_SHOW / CANCELLED |
| `entry_log` | append-only: facility, user type, stay/member/visitor ref, pax, **verified by** (stay lookup, member verify / RFID / QR from 2.7), charge posted (2.5) or benefit used (2.7 redemption), staff, at |

**Venues** (Vedika Hall, Camelot Hall, lawns, pool deck, gazebo) are kept only for **status/closure and location** (and as ticket locations). **Event booking is not in the core scope** (scope gap, Q-104).

### 3.2 Entry flow
Staff open **Facility check-in** (phone layout) → choose the facility → identify the user (room no. → in-house stay, member verify, or day visitor) → the ERP checks **status, hours, rule, capacity/session, age/safety acknowledgement** → shows *Allowed · Free / ₹X / Benefit available* → staff log the entry → a charge to the folio or direct payment (2.5), or a 2.7 benefit redemption (e.g. a day-outing coupon) → done.

**Day visitors:** only if the client confirms a day-visitor or day-out product (C-05, Q-113). Until then the DAY_VISITOR rule is **off**.

### 3.3 Screens
**Facility board** (status, today's hours, occupancy against capacity, sessions), **Facility check-in** (phone), **Session schedule** per facility, **Facility settings** (hours, rules, prices, safety text).

## 4. Spa (D-176)

| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| `spa_service` | name, category, duration_min, buffer_min, price_paise, tax category SPA (2.5), skills needed, room type needed, active |
| `therapist` | staff member link, skills, weekly roster + exceptions (leave, extra shifts) |
| `treatment_room` | name, kind (single / couple), status (linked to facility MAINTENANCE) |
| `spa_appointment` | guest / member / stay ref, service, therapist, room, **time range**, status BOOKED → CONFIRMED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED / NO_SHOW / CANCELLED, source (DESK / PHONE / WALK_IN), price, discount (2.7: C13 20 % therapies, V14 spa 1+1), notes |
| `spa_health_note` | short contraindication flag + note. **Sensitive (health data):** visible only with `spa.health_notes_read`, kept for a set period (Q-114), never in events or logs |

- **No double booking:** Postgres **exclusion constraints** on (therapist, time range) and (room, time range) while the status is active. This needs the `btree_gist` extension, approved in the ADR. Concurrency test: two desks booking the same slot → one wins.
- The booking checks the therapist's roster and skills, the room type, and spa opening hours.
- **Completion → charge** to the stay's folio (2.5) or direct payment; the membership discount is applied as a 2.7 redemption. **No-show / late cancel** fee per policy (Q-114).
- **Screens:** an **appointment book** (day view, one column per therapist, rooms shown), a quick walk-in, a therapist **My day** phone view, and spa settings (catalogue, roster, rooms).

## 10. Verification (when executed)
1. **SLA:**
   - a P1 ticket not answered in 15 min → escalated to the supervisor, then to the GM;
   - ON_HOLD pauses the clock;
   - a reopened ticket keeps its history;
   - automatic close after N hours;
   - all timings tested in IST.
2. **Room impact:** "can't sell room" → OOS straight away; OOO request → 2.3 saga → OTA mock availability −1; resolve → block released → room DIRTY → housekeeping task created.
3. **Guest request:** towels for room 104 → appears in the housekeeper's phone view → done → on the guest's CRM timeline.
4. **PM:** a daily plan creates exactly one ticket per day (job run twice → still one); a failed checklist item raises a follow-up ticket; a missed PM shows in the report.
5. **Vendor:** a quote over the limit can't be accepted without approval; the cost appears in the report.
6. **Facilities:**
   - a closed facility refuses entry;
   - a member allowed by tier → entry logged with RFID/QR verification;
   - a session at capacity refuses the 11th person;
   - an included benefit is used once (2.7);
   - a paid entry posts one charge (idempotent).
7. **Spa:** two desks book the same therapist/slot at once → one succeeds (exclusion constraint); outside the roster → refused; completion → one folio charge with the member discount; health notes hidden from roles without the permission and absent from logs.
8. **Permission tests** for every endpoint. All service tests, web tests, lint and build pass.
