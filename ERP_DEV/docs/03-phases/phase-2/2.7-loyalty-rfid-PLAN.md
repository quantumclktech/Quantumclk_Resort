## 1. Decisions (D-170 … D-175)

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-170 | **Two loyalty engines: the booklet (vouchers + coupons) and points.** The booklet engine tracks what the resort actually sells. The points engine covers the SOW (earn, redeem, history, adjustments, tiers) but **ships switched off** (earn rate 0, feature flag) until the client gives rules (Q-101). | Ashish chose the recommended option. Matches both the SOW and the client's product, with no invented rates. |
| D-171 | **Validity is a setting per booklet edition:** a fixed period (the **2027 edition = 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2027**, as printed) or N months from activation. The membership itself stays **12 months from verification** until the client confirms. Coupons (no printed dates) follow the booklet edition's validity. | Ashish chose the recommended option. The printed paper and the ERP rule disagree, so the client decides (Q-097). |
| D-172 | **Stopping reuse: a balance per member per benefit kind + the paper leaf is collected.** The ERP holds e.g. "Breakfast 10 → 9". At redemption, staff identify the member (RFID tap or member no.), the ERP takes one off **atomically**, and staff collect and cancel the leaf. Works with the booklets already printed. | Ashish chose the recommended option. The leaves have no serials; a reprint isn't needed. |
| D-173 | **Tiers are configurable, starting with Gold.** A tier has a price, validity rule, booklet edition, points multiplier and qualification (bought and/or earned). Launch has one tier, **Gold**. | Ashish chose the recommended option. Plan names and fees aren't settled (Q-030, Q-102). |
| D-174 | **RFID cards: NTAG 424 DNA** with **SUN** (a fresh AES-CMAC-signed, encrypted tap code with a counter) **checked on the server**, so a copied card or replayed tap is refused. Per-card keys are derived from a server-held master key. The card technology is also put to the client to confirm (Q-103). | Ashish chose option 1 and asked to keep it as a client question. The SOW asks for "RFID authentication" and "secure enrolment". |
| D-175 | **No door locks; verify only.** Cards identify and verify members at the **one reception terminal** (enrol, verify, renew, redeem). Facility staff (pool, spa, gym) check entitlements in the ERP. Door-lock integration would be a change request. | Ashish chose the recommended option. SOW excludes physical access-control infrastructure; only 1 terminal is included. |

**For Ashish (not the client), settled in ADR unit L1:** where membership lives. Today it is inside `access-service` (an open question already, Q-028). **Proposal:** a new **`loyalty-service`** owning membership, tiers, booklets, points and RFID cards. Existing membership tables move there through a planned data migration, and access-service goes back to employees only.

## 3. Booklet engine (vouchers + coupons)

### 3.1 Model
| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| `benefit_kind` | code (V1…V14, C1…C13 per the extract), title as printed, group (STAY, DAY_OUTING, BREAKFAST_FNB, A_LA_CARTE, BEVERAGE, CELEBRATION, SPA), leaf type VOUCHER/COUPON, **effect** (see 3.2), parameters, **redeem_at** outlets, rules (3.3), estimated value_paise (Q-100) |
| `booklet_edition` | code `GHR-2027`, validity (fixed 2027-01-01..2027-12-31 or N months), print file reference + sha256, lines kind × qty (**27 lines, 180 leaves**, seeded from the extract), status |
| `member_booklet` | member, edition, issued_at, valid_from/to (worked out at issue), status ACTIVE/EXPIRED/VOID, `booklet_no` = membership number (sheet) |
| `member_benefit_balance` | member_booklet, kind, **issued**, **used**, sub-buckets where printed (C5–C7: LUNCH 5 / DINNER 5), version. **DB check: used ≤ issued** |
| `redemption` | balance row, qty, outlet, member identified by (RFID_TAP / MEMBER_NO_ID_CHECK / MEMBER_QR), bill/folio ref, discount_paise, leaf_collected (bool), staff, at, status REDEEMED / REVERSED (reason, approved_by ≠ staff) |

### 3.2 Effects (how each printed benefit works)
| Effect | Used by | System behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| FREE_NIGHT | V1 | A room night at ₹0 on a reservation through a voucher rate override (2.1), stock still consumed. Booked at the desk |
| NTH_NIGHT_DISCOUNT (n=2, 100 % / 50 %) | V2, V3 | Needs ≥ 2 paid nights in the same stay. The 2nd night's charge gets a discount line |
| FREE_ITEM | V4 day outing, V5 breakfast | Discount line = the item price on the folio/bill |
| AMOUNT_CREDIT (₹1,000) | V6 | Discount up to ₹1,000 on a bill. Whether the rest can carry over is a client question (Q-098) |
| PERCENT_OFF (min bill) | V7 (50 %, min ₹4,000), C8 (40 % beverages), C13 (20 % therapies), C2 (50 %, max 10 entries) | Percentage on eligible lines; minimum bill and entry limit checked |
| BOGO (buy N get N) | C1, C3, C4, C5–C7, C9–C12 (beer 2+2), V14 | Discount = price of the cheapest N eligible items |
| EVENT_BENEFIT | V8–V13 | Events/banquets are **not in the ERP core scope**: the redemption is recorded by hand against an event reference (Q-104) |

### 3.3 Rules (all settings; placeholders until Q-098)
Validity window (D-171) · outlet allowed · meal period (lunch/dinner buckets) · minimum bill · maximum entries · advance booking for stay vouchers (placeholder 48 h, from the draft website text) · blackout dates/weekends · one leaf per bill per kind · combinable with other offers (default **no**) · who may use (member present / family / transferable; default **member present with card or ID**).

### 3.4 Redemption flow (D-172)
1. **Identify the member:** tap the card on the terminal (SUN checked, §5), or enter the member no. + check photo ID, or scan the member QR on the website card.
2. Staff choose the benefit. The ERP shows the balance and the rules for this outlet.
3. **Check:** membership ACTIVE, inside the validity window, balance > 0 (and the right bucket), rules pass.
4. **In one transaction:** `UPDATE balance SET used = used + 1 WHERE id = ? AND used < issued` (0 rows → 409 "none left") + redemption row + outbox event.
5. billing posts the **discount line** to the folio/bill (2.5 charge category DISCOUNT, sourceRef = redemption id, idempotent).
6. Staff **collect the paper leaf** and tick "leaf collected". Uncollected leaves appear in a daily exceptions list.
7. **Reversal** (mistake or cancelled bill): reason + manager approval (≠ the redeeming staff), balance restored, discount reversed in billing.

### 3.5 Issuing booklets
On verification (or renewal) the ERP creates the member's booklet from the current edition. Staff hand over the physical booklet with the member no. written or stamped on the leaves (Q-099). The issue is recorded with the staff member and time.

## 5. RFID (ERP-06, §20A)

### 5.1 Hardware (QuantumCLK supplies, §20A)
| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Terminal (1) | **ESP32-S3** + **PN532-class NFC reader** (ISO 14443-4 / ISO-DEP for NTAG 424 DNA), small display, buzzer/LED, Wi-Fi to the ERP over HTTPS, enclosure + power. Exact parts are chosen in the L1 spike (PN532 vs PN5180) |
| Cards (100) | **NTAG 424 DNA**, printed with the resort branding (artwork from the client, Q-103). Every card is logged in an inventory (received, personalised, issued, spare) |
| Additional terminals/cards | priced as add-ons (SOW); not built in |

### 5.2 Card security (D-174)
- **Personalisation at enrolment:** the factory keys are replaced with **per-card AES keys derived from a master key held only on the server** (key derivation from the card UID).
  - The terminal **relays** the commands between the server and the card, so **keys never sit on the terminal**.
  - SUN is switched on: every tap gives encrypted card data + a counter + a CMAC.
- **Checking a tap:** the terminal sends the SUN data. The server decrypts it, checks the CMAC, and checks the **counter is higher than the last seen** (stops replay and clones). It then maps the card to the member and checks the status.
- **Key management:** the master key lives in the secret store (never in the repo or DB), with a key version per card so keys can be rotated.
- Exact command sequence and libraries: an **L1 spike** proves the relay on real cards before the rest is built.

### 5.3 Terminal security and firmware
- Firmware in the repo (`firmware/rfid-terminal`, ESP-IDF; a new technology for the repo, recorded in an ADR).
- **Secure boot + flash encryption** on the ESP32. **Signed OTA updates**.
- **Device identity:** a per-device credential issued at commissioning (mTLS client certificate or a device token kept in encrypted flash). HTTPS only.
- **Modes:** ENROL (blank card → member chosen on the ERP screen), VERIFY (tap → member name, tier, status, validity on the ERP screen + green/red on the terminal), REPLACE.
- **No offline verification:** if the server can't be reached, the terminal shows "offline" and staff use member no. + ID.

### 5.4 Backend (loyalty-service)
| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| `rfid_card` | uid (not secret), key_version, status BLANK → ASSIGNED → ACTIVE ↔ SUSPENDED → LOST / REPLACED / RETIRED, member, last_counter, issued_at/by, deposit_paise (C-15: none until confirmed), replacement fee (Q-103) |
| `rfid_card_event` | append-only: enrolled, activated, suspended, lost, replaced (→ new card), retired; actor, reason |
| `rfid_device` | terminal id, location, status, firmware version, credential fingerprint, last seen |
| `access_log` | append-only: card or member, device or web user, purpose (VERIFY, REDEEM, CHECKIN, RENEWAL, FACILITY_POOL / SPA / GYM …), result OK / DENIED (reason: expired, suspended, unknown card, replayed tap, bad CMAC), at |

- **Card status follows the membership:** an expired membership → the tap shows EXPIRED; renewal reactivates the same card.
- **Lost card:** suspended at once → a replacement card is enrolled → the old one is RETIRED. History is kept.

### 5.5 Facility verification without doors (D-175)
Pool, spa and gym staff open **"Verify member"** in the ERP (phone or tablet): enter the member no., or **scan the member QR** on the website card (a signed, short-lived QR), or send the member to the reception terminal. The ERP shows status, tier and entitlements and writes an `access_log` row with the facility. Which facilities need member checks: Q-105.

### 5.6 Commissioning (§20A)
Install at reception → Wi-Fi + device credential → test script (enrol 3 cards, verify, replay attempt refused, clone attempt refused, lost + replace, expired member refused) → personalise the 100 cards in batches → handover document (setup, troubleshooting, spare cards, key rotation steps) → client sign-off.

## 10. Verification (when executed)
1. **Booklet maths:**
   - the 2027 edition seeds exactly 180 leaves in 27 kinds;
   - 20 parallel redemptions of a kind with 1 left → exactly 1 succeeds;
   - lunch/dinner buckets kept separate;
   - outside the validity window → refused;
   - a reversal restores the balance and the discount.
2. **Effects:**
   - 2nd-night-free needs 2 paid nights;
   - BOGO discounts the cheaper item;
   - the 50 % à la carte voucher refuses a bill under ₹4,000;
   - a day-outing coupon caps at 10 entries;
   - every discount appears on the folio exactly once (idempotent).
3. **Points (flag on in tests):** earn on settle; a credit note reverses the earn; redeem as payment; FIFO expiry; an adjustment needs approval; the ledger can't be updated or deleted.
4. **RFID security:**
   - a genuine tap → OK;
   - the **same SUN message replayed → DENIED (replay)**;
   - a card with the right UID but wrong keys (clone) → DENIED (bad CMAC);
   - a suspended or expired member → DENIED;
   - the terminal has no master key in flash (checked on the device image);
   - firmware updates must be signed.
5. **Lifecycle:** verify → booklet issued → card enrolled → redeem breakfast at the restaurant → expiry reminder → expiry → tap shows EXPIRED → renewal → new booklet, same card reactivated → lost card → replacement works and the old card is refused.
6. **§20A acceptance:** the commissioning test script passes on site, all 100 cards are in the inventory, and the client signs off.
7. All service tests, firmware unit tests (host-side), web and landing tests, lint and build pass.
