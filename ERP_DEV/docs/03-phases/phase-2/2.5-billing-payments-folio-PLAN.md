**Design rules that don't change** (engineering rules #1, #4, #5, #6):
- Money is stored as **integer paise**, INR.
- There is one tax and rounding module.
- **Nothing financial is ever deleted or edited.** Charges are corrected with adjustment lines. Invoices are corrected with **credit notes**.
- Every payment, refund and webhook is idempotent.
- **No card data touches our system.** The gateway uses hosted checkout and the terminal reads the card.
- Every money action is audited.

## 4. Tax engine (one module, tests first)

1. Each charge gets its tax rule by **category + per-unit slab + business date** (effective dating, so a rate change never alters past bills).
2. **Room slab:** the per-room-per-night tariff after discount decides the slab. This needs the CA's confirmation of which value counts (Q-073).
3. **Intra-state** (the guest's place of supply in Karnataka, or B2C): CGST + SGST, half each. **Inter-state B2B** with a GSTIN from another state: IGST. Place-of-supply rules are confirmed by the CA.
4. **Rounding:** each tax line half-up to the paise. A configurable invoice **round-off** to the nearest rupee is shown as a separate line.
5. Rates are **inclusive or exclusive** per rule, because published tariffs may be quoted inclusive.
6. **Tests:** every slab edge (₹7,500.00 vs ₹7,500.01 in the placeholder data), a discount moving a room across a slab, inclusive back-calculation, CGST/SGST vs IGST, and a rate change effective mid-stay (each night taxed by its own date).

## 5. Flows

### 5.1 Posting charges (from every module)
`POST /internal/v1/folios/charges {stayRef|folioId, category, sourceModule, sourceRef, qty, unitPaise, description}` (service token).
- **Idempotent** by (sourceModule, sourceRef), so a replayed event never charges twice.
- Routing rules pick the target folio. Tax is calculated and the lines are written in one transaction.
- Sources at launch: the night audit (room + extra bed, per night), early/late check-out (2.4), minibar (2.4), cancellation / no-show (from reservation events). Dining, spa and facilities use the same API later.
- **Adjustments and discounts** are new lines with `reversal_of`. Above the 2.2 limits they need approval. A reason is always required.

### 5.2 Online advance (booking engine; the guest UI comes in a later plan)
1. reservation-service creates a **HOLD** (2.1) with an expiry from `advance_policy`.
2. billing creates a gateway **order / payment link** for the advance amount. **The amount always comes from the server.**
3. The guest pays on the gateway's hosted page. A **webhook** arrives, its signature is verified, it is deduplicated in `provider_event`, and the payment becomes SUCCEEDED → `PaymentSucceeded` → the reservation becomes CONFIRMED.
4. If the hold expires unpaid, a job marks the payment EXPIRED, and reservation-service releases the stock. A late webhook for an expired hold → payment SUCCEEDED + "needs attention" (confirm if stock is free, otherwise refund).
5. If a webhook is lost, the job asks the gateway for the payment status, so the result never depends only on the webhook.

### 5.3 Desk payment through the Pine Labs terminal (D-159)
1. The cashier opens a shift (D-160), selects the folio and amount, and chooses the terminal.
2. billing creates the payment (INITIATED, idempotency key = payment id) and **pushes the sale** to the terminal through the adapter.
3. The guest taps, inserts the card or scans UPI **on the terminal**. Our system never sees the card.
4. The result arrives by callback or status poll → SUCCEEDED / FAILED, with the terminal txn id and RRN stored.
5. If a request **times out**, billing queries the terminal status before allowing a retry, so a guest is never charged twice.
6. **Void** (same day, before settlement) and **refund** go through the terminal adapter.
7. **Fallback:** if the terminal integration is down, `payments.manual_record` (sensitive) lets staff record method + terminal txn id + optional proof image (same rules as membership). These payments are flagged for reconciliation.
8. **Cash:** recorded against the open shift, with change given noted.

### 5.4 Check-out settlement (replaces the 2.4 stub)
- `FolioPort.balance(stay)` returns the real balance across the stay's folios.
- **Settle** means pay the balance (gateway / terminal / cash) **or** move the routed charges to a company account (credit limit checked) **or** mark them OTA-collect (2.1).
- The **final tax invoice** is issued for the guest folio, plus a separate invoice for the company folio if needed. The folio becomes SETTLED → `FolioSettled` → front office can check the guest out. The PDF is available to print or email (email through the notification service later).
- An **interim bill** during the stay is a **proforma**, not a tax invoice, and has no number from the series.
- The GM override (D-157) records `SETTLEMENT_OVERRIDDEN` with a reason. The folio stays open for Accounts.

### 5.5 Refunds
Request (reason) → the **approval policy** (2.2 R7 thresholds, Q-052/Q-081) → approved by someone other than the requester → **execute**: gateway refund API, terminal refund, or cash paid out from an open shift → status tracked to SUCCEEDED/FAILED → a credit note if an invoice was already issued. Partial refunds are allowed, and the total can never exceed what was paid (DB check).

### 5.6 Cancellation and no-show
reservation-service publishes `ReservationCancelled` / `NoShowMarked` with the times. billing works out the fee from `cancellation_policy`, posts it as a charge, keeps that part of the advance, and offers a refund of the rest (through §5.5). OTA bookings follow the OTA's own policy as sent (2.1).

### 5.7 Company accounts and OTA receivables
- At check-out the routed charges are invoiced to the company (B2B with its GSTIN). An `ar_entry` is created with a due date. If the credit limit would be exceeded → blocked, unless a manager approves.
- When the company pays (bank transfer / cheque / gateway), the payment is allocated to entries, part payments included.
- **OTA_COLLECT** bookings: the gross amount becomes a receivable from the OTA account, with the commission from the 2.1 rule. Payouts are matched by settlement reference.
- Reports: outstanding by company, **ageing** 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days.

### 5.8 Cashier shift close
Close shift → expected totals per method (cash, terminal card, terminal UPI, manual) → the cashier enters the counted cash → variance = counted − expected. A non-zero variance needs a reason and **manager approval**. The shift report can be printed. A shift can't be reopened; corrections are adjustments in the next shift.

### 5.9 Reconciliation dashboard
- Daily: payments by method; gateway **settlement report** (imported via API or CSV) and terminal settlement matched to ERP payments; unmatched items; open shifts; shift variances; refunds waiting; webhooks not processed; manual-record payments still to verify.
- Accounts mark each item resolved, with a note.

## 9. Security

- **PCI:** hosted checkout and terminal only. No PAN, CVV or expiry is ever received or stored. For virtual cards (2.1), only the token and last 4 digits are kept.
- **Webhooks and callbacks:** signature check, timestamp tolerance, IP allowlist, dedupe, and a fast 200 response with processing in a worker.
- **Secrets:** gateway and terminal keys come from environment/secret config only (rule #16). Sandbox and live keys are separate per environment.
- Amounts are always worked out on the server. A client-sent amount is only a request, checked against the folio balance.
- An audit row for every money action, with before/after.

## 12. Verification (when executed)

1. **Tax engine:**
   - every slab edge;
   - inclusive back-calculation;
   - CGST/SGST vs IGST;
   - a mid-stay rate change;
   - discount across a slab;
   - totals match to the paise;
   - round-off line.
2. **Idempotency:** the same charge event 5× → one charge; the same webhook 5× → one payment; a terminal timeout then retry → status queried first, one sale only.
3. **Gap-free invoices:** 20 parallel settlements → consecutive numbers, no gaps or duplicates. Invoices can't be updated or deleted (trigger); corrections only through credit notes.
4. **Refund rules:** refund > paid → refused; requester = approver → 409; above the threshold without approval → 409.
5. **Shift:** a close with a variance and no reason → refused; a cashier approving their own variance → 409.
6. **Company credit:** a check-out over the credit limit → blocked without approval; a part payment allocation leaves the right outstanding amount and ageing bucket.
7. **Security:** a forged webhook signature → 401 and no state change; logs and the DB contain no card data; a client-sent amount different from the folio → refused.
8. **Live demo on the mocks:**
   - walk-in → night audit posts the room night → minibar posted → take payment on the **mock terminal** → late check-out fee → settle → **tax invoice PDF** → refund part → credit note;
   - an online booking hold → mock gateway webhook → CONFIRMED; an expired hold → stock released;
   - shift close with a variance;
   - reconciliation import with one deliberate mismatch.
9. All service tests, web tests, lint and build pass.
